How the local game works

      

        Each week, the retailer, wholesaler, distributor and factory take one private turn. Every player sees
        only their local inventory, backlog, incoming order, incoming shipment, known supply line and local history.
      

      

        Orders travel upstream with a two-week information delay. Shipments and factory production travel
        downstream with a two-week physical delay. All decisions are applied simultaneously after the factory submits.
      

      

        Demand, orders, shipments and production begin at four cases per week, with zero backlog. Before starting,
        the facilitator can set a separate initial inventory for each echelon. The demand scenario remains hidden during play.
      

      

        Inventory costs 0.5 cost units per case per week, while backlog costs 1.0 cost unit per case per week.
